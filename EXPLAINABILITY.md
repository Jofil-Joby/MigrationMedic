## Decision and Reasoning

MigrationMedic makes an assessment by analyzing evidence related to database migrations. It connects detected problems to supporting evidence and practical actions.

## Inputs and Data Sources

MigrationMedic uses source files, configuration, project structure, and relevant database migrations data from the inspected project.

## Limits and Constraints

MigrationMedic is limited when required information is missing, inaccessible, generated dynamically, or incomplete.
